One Platform. Every School Operation. Smarter with AI.

School Shabdaa is an AI-powered school management platform designed to simplify school administration, improve parent–teacher communication, and bring academic and operational activities together in one place.

🌐 Website: https://www.schoolshabdaa.com/

🚀 Key Features

🤖 Generative AI: AI-powered assistance for school administrators, teachers, parents, and students.

🎓 Academic Management: Manage academic activities, student records, timetables, and assessments.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent–Teacher Communication: Enable seamless communication between parents and teachers, including voice messages and recordings.

💳 Online Fee Payments: Simplify fee collection through integrated payment gateways.

🚌 School Bus Tracking: Help parents track school transportation and improve visibility into student commutes.

📊 School Analytics: Support data-driven decisions through reports and insights.

🔔 Notifications: Keep parents and staff informed about important school updates.

🏢 Administrative Automation: Reduce repetitive administrative work and improve operational efficiency.

🎯 Our Mission

To help schools spend less time managing operations and more time improving student learning, teacher productivity, and parent engagement.
